The Artist

About

Add erikka.jpg to show photo

Erikka Miles is an artist and educator working across painting, drawing, and sculpture in Los Angeles.

She teaches art for the Los Angeles Unified School District, where her classes have spanned Intro to Art, Drawing, Painting, and Sculpture — guiding students into the disciplines that shape her own practice.

Her foundation is in education: she holds a Bachelor of Education from California State University, Los Angeles. Now a prospective MFA student, Erikka is sharpening a body of work that bridges the classroom and the studio.

  • Teaching Art teacher at LAUSD — Intro to Art, Drawing, Painting, and Sculpture.
  • Education Bachelor of Education, California State University, Los Angeles.
  • Next Prospective MFA student.
